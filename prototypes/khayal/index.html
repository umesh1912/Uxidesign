Khayal: your parents' medicines, from wherever you are

You are Karan, working in Pune. Your parents live in Nagpur. Khayal reminds them to take their medicines, and it tells you, and a nearby neighbour if needed, when a dose is missed. Medicines and readings are sample data. This is not medical advice.

1Handle a missed dose. Mummy's 2 PM calcium wasn't confirmed. Open Mummy's day on Today and choose what to do.
2See the parent's side. With Mummy selected, tap "Parent's phone" for the big-button view. Try Hindi, then tap "I took it".
3Scan a prescription. Add a medicine, attach any photo, and review what the scan found.
4Order a refill. Telmisartan runs out in 9 days. Send the list to the chemist.
5Prepare for the doctor. On Health, log a BP reading and open the doctor summary.
Planned build: Flutter app with two modes (caregiver and parent), Firebase, exact-time local alarms, and push alerts to family.
