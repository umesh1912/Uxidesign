How data moves: consent first

Every access is granted, scoped, time-bound and logged
Patient→Doctor
Patient grants access, the doctor reviews the history needed for the visit.
Patient→Insurer
Patient authorises only the records a specific claim needs, nothing more.
Doctor→Pharmacy
Doctor issues an e-prescription, the patient's chosen pharmacy receives it.
