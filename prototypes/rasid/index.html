Rasid: every bill, every warranty, found in seconds

You are Priya in Hyderabad. Rasid keeps the bill for every appliance and gadget in the house, tells you before a warranty runs out, and gets a repair claim ready in one tap. All items are sample data.

1Catch an expiring warranty. The mixer grinder's warranty ends in 7 days. Open it from Home.
2Start a claim. Pick the problem, and Rasid assembles the invoice number, serial and dates to send.
3Scan a new bill. Tap "Scan bill", attach any image, and check what was read.
4Follow a repair. The washing machine claim is waiting for a part. See its timeline under Claims.
5Set up forwarding. Under You, copy your forwarding address for online-order emails.
Planned build: Flutter, Firebase, on-device text recognition plus a cloud invoice parser, and email-forward ingestion.
