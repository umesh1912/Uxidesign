Jodi: habits stick when someone's watching

A habit tracker built for two. You and one partner see each other's check-ins, cheer or nudge, and share a light weekly pact. Missing a day is allowed; missing twice isn't. You are Ishaan in Mumbai, paired with your friend Tara in Delhi. All data is sample data.

1Check in. Tick "Read 20 pages" on Today, add a note, and see Tara's side light up.
2Cheer or nudge. On Tara's tab, react to her meditation and nudge her about guitar.
3See the pact. This week, the one below 80% buys chai. Watch the bars move.
4Add a habit with a cue: "After I brush my teeth, I will floss."
5Find a new partner under You, matched by goal, time zone and language.
Planned build: Flutter, Firebase with real-time partner sync, Cloud Functions for streak rules and nudges, and push notifications with quiet hours.
