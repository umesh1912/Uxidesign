Hariyali: plant care that checks the weather and the soil

Hariyali tells you which plants need water today, and skips balcony plants when rain is coming. It asks you to check the soil first, because overwatering is a common way houseplants die. It identifies plants and problems from a photo, and writes care instructions for whoever looks after your plants while you're away. You are Meenakshi in Chennai. Plants, weather and scan results are sample data.

1Water with a check. Tap "Water" on the tulsi. Hariyali asks whether the soil is dry first.
2See the rain skip. The curry leaf is due, but 18 mm of rain is expected tomorrow.
3Diagnose. On Scan, attach any photo to see a sample diagnosis of mealybugs on the hibiscus.
4Going away? On Away, make a care note for your neighbour in Tamil or English.
5Pets at home? Turn on pet safety under You to flag plants that aren't safe for cats and dogs.
Planned build: Flutter, Firebase, a plant identification model, and a weather forecast API that adjusts watering by location.
