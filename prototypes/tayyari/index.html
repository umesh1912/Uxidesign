Tayyari: a study buddy that knows what you're about to forget

Upload your notes, and Tayyari turns them into practice questions in Hindi or English. It then schedules revision just before you'd forget each fact, and plans your day around your weakest topics. You are Aman, preparing for SSC CGL. The questions and scores are sample data.

1Do today's revision. Answer the questions due today, then rate how easy each felt.
2Switch to Hindi. Tap हिंदी at the top. Polity questions switch language.
3Turn notes into questions. Tap "Notes", attach any file, and review the questions made from it.
4Find weak spots. Syllabus shows which topics cost you the most marks.
5Read your mock. Progress shows where marks were lost and which mistakes were confident ones.
Planned build: Flutter, Firebase, an LLM for question generation with source checks, and an FSRS-style revision scheduler on the device.
