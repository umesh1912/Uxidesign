Hisaab: rent, bills and chores for flatmates

You are signed in as Anu, sharing Flat 3B with Rohan, Priya and Karthik. All data is sample data. Your changes stay in this browser until you reset them.

Flexible splitsSplit equally, by exact amounts, or by shares. Attach a receipt photo.
Recurring billsRent, Wi-Fi and maid salary come due on Home. Add one with a tap.
Shared shopping listTick off what you bought and turn it into an expense.
Fair choresSwap turns, mark someone away, and compare against a fair share.
UPI settle-upEach payment shows the UPI link the real app would open.
Search and trendsFilter bills by category or person and see three months of spend.
