Galli: what's happening in your lane, right now

A feed of alerts and lost-and-found posts from people within a kilometre of you. Neighbours confirm alerts so rumours don't spread, and lost pets get sightings pinned on a map. You are Rhea in Kothrud, Pune. Every post is sample data.

1Confirm an alert. Tap "Still on" on the power cut, and watch it move from unverified to confirmed.
2Help find Bruno. Open the lost dog, add a sighting, and see the Lost & found match.
3See the map. Pins show every post within the radius. Change the radius to 2 km.
4Post something. Report a found item. Your exact location is never shown.
5Check the safety post. The chain-snatching report stays labelled "unverified" until others confirm.
Planned build: Flutter, Firebase with geohash queries, Cloud Functions for fan-out and moderation, and push notifications by area.
