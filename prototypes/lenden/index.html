Lenden: teach what you know, learn what you don't

Neighbours swap lessons instead of paying for them. Every hour you teach earns one time credit, and every hour you learn spends one. You are signed in as Neha in HSR Layout, Bengaluru. Everyone else is a sample profile.

1Find a match. Turn on "Wants my skills" on Discover to see people who want what you teach.
2Request a swap. Open Vikram, pick guitar, offer Excel in return, and choose a time.
3Answer a request. Deepa wants Excel lessons in exchange for yoga. Accept it under Swaps.
4Run a session. Start one, show the check-in code, mark it done and rate it. Watch your credits change.
5Join a circle. Book a seat at the Sunday chess circle for one credit.
Planned build: Flutter, Firebase Auth (phone OTP), Cloud Firestore with geohash search, Cloud Functions for the credit ledger, and Cloud Messaging.
