Utsav: one place for the whole family to plan the wedding

Guests and RSVPs by event, tasks shared out among relatives, the budget against what's been paid, and every vendor's balance and due date. You are Anjali, the bride's sister, organising Riya and Kabir's wedding in Jaipur. All names and amounts are sample data.

1Chase RSVPs. On Guests, send WhatsApp reminders to families who haven't replied.
2Get the caterer's count. Pick the Sangeet and share veg and Jain headcounts.
3Hand out a task. Add a task and assign it to Mama ji. Relatives only see their own tasks.
4Watch the budget. Catering and decor are over estimate. Record the next payment to the decorator.
5Allot rooms. Put out-of-town families in the 20 hotel rooms automatically.
Planned build: Flutter, Firebase with shared event workspaces and roles, WhatsApp RSVP links, and a web RSVP page for guests without the app.
