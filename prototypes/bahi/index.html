Bahi: the udhaar register, in your pocket and in your language

A credit ledger for kirana shops and small vendors. Say or type "Ramesh 340 udhaar", and it's written in the right customer's khata. Bahi also shows who to collect from first and sends polite WhatsApp reminders with a UPI ID. The interface is Hindi first, with English one tap away. You are Rakesh Gupta in Indore. All customers are sample data.

1Write by voice. Tap the yellow "बोलकर लिखें" button (speak an entry) and confirm what it heard.
2Type it instead. In the same box, type "Salim 200 jama" and see it read as a payment.
3Collect. Open वसूली (collections), select the top three, and send reminders.
4Open a khata. Tap Mohan Chai Wala to see his running balance, then record a payment.
5Switch to English using the button at the top.
Planned build: Flutter, offline-first local database with Firebase sync, on-device Hindi speech recognition, and WhatsApp Business messages.
